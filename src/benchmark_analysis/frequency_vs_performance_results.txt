Frequency vs. performance -- Pooled Spearman correlations
============================================================

Each point is one (model, entity) pair -- a template/identity/role's
ground-truth frequency in the benchmark, repeated once per model and
paired with that model's own score (Jaccard for templates, F1 for
identity/role labels). This matches the 'Pooled Spearman' statistics
reported in the paper text (as opposed to correlating frequency
against each entity's mean score across models).

1. Meme template frequency vs. per-image Jaccard (n=9971, model x image pairs)
   Spearman rho = -0.0069, p = 0.4886

2. Social identity frequency vs. F1 (n=369, model x label pairs)
   Spearman rho = 0.3521, p = 3.291e-12

3. Narrative role frequency vs. F1 (n=81, model x label pairs)
   Spearman rho = 0.2940, p = 0.007726
